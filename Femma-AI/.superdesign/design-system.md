# UNSTOPPABLE mobile design system

## Product context

UNSTOPPABLE is a women-first transformation platform that unifies fitness, yoga and recovery, personal safety, nutrition, cycle health, pregnancy and postpartum support. The promise is one journey: beginner to consistent to stronger to calmer to confident to aware to safer to unstoppable.

The product is supportive, premium, confidence-building and safety-conscious. It must never feel like disconnected mini-apps. Explore is the discovery and learning layer; Today turns selected courses into a personalized daily mission; Progress records completion, levels, badges and streaks.

Primary mobile frame: 390 x 844 portrait. Preserve the existing five-item bottom navigation: Today, Explore, Coach, Progress, Profile. Explore is the active item in this design task.

## Authoritative video curriculum

The user-supplied Explore curriculum is the source of truth for category, course, module and lesson naming. It supersedes any broader exploratory taxonomy elsewhere in this document.

Use this content model consistently:

- Category = the top-level Explore grouping.
- Course = a subcategory and a progressive video-learning path.
- Module = a stage inside a course, normally Foundations, Building and Mastery, or a life-stage phase such as a trimester.
- Lesson = one video with title, runtime, completion state and next-lesson continuity.

The exact Explore tree is:

1. Safety
   - Self-Defense: 3 modules, 12 lessons.
   - Confidence Drills: 3 modules, 9 lessons.
2. Fitness
   - Fat Loss: 3 modules, 9 lessons.
   - Sculpt: 3 modules, 9 lessons.
   - Strength: 3 modules, 9 lessons.
   - Yoga: 3 modules, 9 lessons.
3. Pregnancy, Postpartum and Cycle
   - Cycle Sync: 3 modules, 10 lessons.
   - Pregnancy: trimester-based, 3 modules, 9 lessons.
   - Postpartum: Early Recovery, Rebuilding and Returning to Fitness, 9 lessons.
4. Nutrition
   - Diet: 3 modules, 9 lessons.
   - Meal Scanner: 4 feature-tutorial lessons.
   - Meal Planner: 4 feature-tutorial lessons.
   - Recipes: an open-ended video library grouped into Breakfast; Lunch and Dinner; Snacks; Healthy Swaps and Treats, launching with 4 recipes per group.

Cross-category journey cards:

- New Mom Recovery Journey: Postpartum plus postpartum nutrition plus gentle Yoga.
- Confidence and Safety Journey: Self-Defense Foundations plus the full Confidence Drills course.
- 30-Day Fat Loss Kickstart: Fat Loss plus Diet Foundations plus Meal Planner tutorials.
- Cycle-Aligned Living: Cycle Sync plus phase-matched Fitness and Yoga lessons.

Safety course content must be de-escalation-first and include: educational content only; not a substitute for in-person self-defense training. Pregnancy and Postpartum content must include: get clearance from a doctor or midwife before starting or continuing an exercise program.

The full lesson titles, runtimes, module order and rollout guidance are supplied as an additional context file on every curriculum design generation.

## Explore information architecture

### Landing hierarchy

1. Header: Explore title, short supportive line, saved action.
2. Search: one global search across courses, programs, sessions, lessons and recipes.
3. Continue learning: compact card for the active course with progress and next lesson.
4. For you: personalized recommendation rail using goals, time, experience and current cycle/pregnancy state.
5. Browse by category: five primary product pillars, each with a distinct pastel family and useful count.
6. Popular courses: cross-pillar course cards with level, duration or lesson count, rating/progress and premium state.
7. Recently viewed and Saved should be secondary destinations, not competing category pillars.

### Navigation depth

Explore landing -> Pillar hub -> Subcategory browse or course collection -> Course detail -> Lesson detail/player -> Completion and next lesson. Search results, Recently viewed, Saved, Recommended for you and All filters are shared browse utilities.

Every pillar hub should include: an introductory hero, Continue section if applicable, subcategory chips or tiles, recommended learning paths, popular courses, short standalone sessions, and a safety/medical note where relevant.

## Course content model

Every course card must show: title, pillar, cover/illustration, level, total duration or number of lessons, short outcome, premium/free state, and either progress or rating/social proof. Use one primary metadata row and avoid tiny clutter.

Every course detail page must show:
- Pillar breadcrumb and save/share actions.
- Clear title, outcome statement and inclusive visual.
- Course facts: level, total time, lessons/modules, equipment or prerequisites.
- Primary CTA: Start course, Continue course or Resume lesson.
- Progress ring when enrolled.
- What you will learn.
- Module accordion with lesson rows, duration, completion and lock state.
- Coach/instructor guidance and appropriate safety/medical disclaimer.
- Related courses within the same pillar.

Lesson rows use four states: complete, current, available and locked premium. A current lesson must be visually obvious without relying only on color.

## Visual foundations

Use only these colors:
- Background #FAFAFC; surface #FFFFFF; foreground #17181C; secondary text #747985.
- Border #EBEDF0; muted/input #F5F5F8.
- Pink #F26BB5; deep pink #D94A9A; sky blue #77CDED; soft cyan #C9F2F6.
- Lavender #B9A7F2; soft lavender #E9E2FC; mint #A9E4D2; coral #FF928F; warm yellow #FFD88A.

Typography: Manrope only. Screen titles 28-32px ExtraBold. Section titles 18-20px Bold. Card titles 15-18px Bold/SemiBold. Body 14-16px Regular/Medium. Metadata 11-13px Medium/SemiBold.

Use an 8-point spacing rhythm with 20px screen gutters. Cards use 16-24px internal padding, 16-24px radii and subtle 1px borders. Pills use full radius. Touch targets are at least 44px. Avoid heavy shadows; use low-opacity tonal surfaces, whitespace and thin borders.

Use pastel gradients only for category identity, selected hero areas and promotional moments. Keep the majority of the UI warm off-white and white. Never introduce purple neon, decorative serif type, black gradients or unrelated colors.

Imagery, when used, should show diverse women with realistic body types, ages, ethnicities, fitness levels, pregnancy stages and postpartum experiences. Avoid sexualized or aggressive imagery. If imagery is unavailable, use refined iconography and abstract pastel shapes instead of fake photos.

## Components and interaction

Reuse the existing rounded category card, filter chip, section header, workout/course card, progress ring and bottom tab patterns. Expand them into a consistent course system rather than inventing an unrelated component language.

Search should support suggested queries and grouped results by Courses, Sessions, Lessons, Recipes and Tools. Filters: pillar, goal, level, duration, equipment, free/premium and life stage. Show active filter count and an easy Clear all action.

Motion: 300-400ms ease-out entrances, subtle 60-80ms stagger, haptic feedback on card selection, success haptic on enrollment/completion. Motion should help hierarchy and never delay navigation.

## Accessibility and content

Maintain strong text contrast and never place small colored text on a similar pastel surface. Pair color with text/icon/state. Respect safe areas and bottom navigation clearance. Keep headings descriptive and scan-friendly.

Voice is supportive, confident, clear, non-judgmental, motivational and safety-conscious. Examples: Find your next step. Continue where you left off. Small steps build unstoppable confidence. Listen to your body and move at your pace. Practice awareness first; escape and seek safety.

Use correct spelling: categories and subcategories.
