Create a complete high-fidelity mobile app design for a women-first fitness, wellness, nutrition, cycle health, pregnancy, confidence, and personal-safety platform.

WORKING APP NAME:
UNSTOPPABLE

CORE BRAND PROMISE:
“One journey: beginner → fit → confident → safe → unstoppable.”

The app must combine:
1. Workout and fitness programs
2. Yoga, breathwork, flexibility, and recovery
3. Self-defense and beginner taekwondo education
4. Food scanning and macro coaching
5. Recipes and meal planning
6. Menstrual-cycle tracking
7. Pregnancy-safe and postpartum programs
8. Progress, levels, challenges, badges, and streaks

Do not design these as disconnected mini-apps. Everything must feel like one personalized transformation journey.

DESIGN DIRECTION

Use the uploaded UI reference as visual inspiration.

Create a soft, modern, feminine sports-tech interface with:
- White and warm off-white main backgrounds
- Pastel pink, lavender, sky blue, aqua, mint, and soft coral accents
- Subtle pink-to-blue gradient backgrounds for promotional areas
- Large rounded cards with 20–28 px corner radii
- Pill-shaped filters and buttons
- Thin, simple rounded icons
- Soft shadows with low opacity
- Large circular progress rings
- Bold black or dark-charcoal headlines
- Muted gray supporting text
- Athlete photo cutouts inside colorful cards
- Generous white space
- Friendly, supportive, premium visual tone
- Minimal visual clutter
- Strong content hierarchy
- Clear 44 px or larger touch targets
- Accessible text contrast

Use diverse women with realistic body types, ages, ethnicities, fitness levels, pregnancy stages, and postpartum experiences. Avoid overly sexualized fitness imagery.

TYPOGRAPHY

Use Manrope, SF Pro Rounded, or a similar modern rounded sans-serif.

Suggested hierarchy:
- Display statistic: 40–48 px, extra bold
- Main screen heading: 28–32 px, bold
- Section heading: 18–20 px, bold
- Card title: 16–18 px, semibold
- Body text: 14–16 px
- Caption and metadata: 12–13 px
